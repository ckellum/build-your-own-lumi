Start with these

Private chat

A ChatGPT-style assistant on your machine

Beginner4–9B model · any 8 GB GPU or 16 GB Mac

Ask, draft, rewrite, brainstorm, with nothing leaving your computer. LM Studio and Jan are one install; Ollama + Open WebUI gives you a web chat for the whole house.

lmstudio.ai · jan.ai · ollama.com

Transcription

Meetings and voice memos, typed and summarized

Beginnera Whisper-class speech model · runs on almost anything

MacWhisper (Mac) and Buzz (Mac, Windows, Linux, free and open source) transcribe offline and can tell speakers apart. Paste the transcript into your local chat for a summary.

Buzz on GitHub

Translate and write

Email, letters and translations, kept private

Beginner4–12B model

Paste into a local chat app to summarize, draft or translate. Gemma and Qwen models handle many languages. Firefox already translates pages on your device.

Your own information

Your documents

Chat with your files and notes

BeginnerIntermediate7–14B model + a small embedding model

AnythingLLM (desktop app) indexes a folder of PDFs and documents and answers questions with quotes. Open WebUI has “Knowledge” collections. Obsidian users have plugins like Copilot and Smart Connections.

How it works: the files are split into passages and turned into numbers (“embeddings”) by a small local model. Your question finds the closest passages, and only those go to the chat model. Ask for quotes so you can check its answers.

anythingllm.com

Photos

Search your photo library by describing it

Intermediatesmall built-in models · no chat model needed

“Dog at the beach.” “Birthday cake.” Immich is a self-hosted photo library with smart search and face grouping, running on your own server or PC. PhotoPrism is another option and can caption photos with a local model.

immich.app

Offline

A model for the plane, the cabin, the basement

Beginnerwhatever your laptop fits

Download a model before you go and everything above works with no signal. Useful for health, money and work documents you'd rather not upload anywhere.

For builders

Coding

A coding helper in your editor

Intermediate7–14B for autocomplete · 20B+ for edits

Continue, Cline, Aider and Zed all work with Ollama or LM Studio. Local is great for autocomplete and quick questions. For agents that change a whole project, frontier models are still far ahead.

Zed: use a local model · Cline: running models locally

Voice

A voice assistant or kiosk, like Lumi

IntermediateAdvancedspeech-to-text + 4–12B model + a voice

Microphone → speech to text → a language model → text to speech → a speaker. The nine-step guide builds one in about a thousand lines of Python, on a laptop, workstation or cloud models.

Home

Talk to your house

IntermediateHome Assistant + a 4–14B model with tool calling

“Turn off the kitchen lights.” “Is the garage open?” Fully local, with your own model. The home automation guide walks through it.

Agents

Let a model use tools (MCP)

Advanced20B+ for dependable tool use

The Model Context Protocol lets a model read files, search and call apps. LM Studio can host MCP tools and asks you to approve each call. Small models often break the tool format or drop arguments, so keep tasks short, tools few, and approve anything that writes.

LM Studio and MCP

For family, learning and access

Learning

A patient tutor that quizzes you

Beginner4–12B model

Paste class notes and ask it to quiz you instead of answering. Local means a child's questions stay at home. Check its facts: small models are confident even when wrong.

Accessibility

Describe images, read aloud

BeginnerIntermediatea small vision model + a voice

Vision models like Gemma describe a photo or a screenshot; a local voice like Kokoro reads text aloud. VOLlama is an accessible app for Ollama.

Kitchen

A hands-free kitchen helper

Intermediatethe minilumi kit's my-day example

Recipes read out step by step, the grocery list, trash day, the Wi-Fi password. The kit ships a made-up household you can try in five minutes.

Two rules for everything here. Check facts that matter: small models can be confidently wrong. And never let a model take an action you can't undo (unlock, delete, send, buy) without asking you first.
